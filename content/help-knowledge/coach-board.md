# Coach Board

The **Coach Board** tab is scoped to **one match at a time**.

1. Pick or search for a fixture in the left match list.
2. Drag players from the home or away **bench** onto that team’s half of the pitch (top = home, bottom = away).
3. Use **Your saved boards** to switch between saved lineups for that match. Drag a match from the list onto saved boards to change fixture.
4. **Save board** when both teams have 11 players placed. **Publish** sends an approved lineup post to the feed.

Tap a bench chip marked “on pitch” to remove a player. Move players on the pitch by dragging tokens between slots.

Player pools come from StatsBomb history, API-Football squads (when configured), and Wikipedia call-up lists.
