# Sign-in, community, and admin

**Sign in** uses Google OAuth from the header. After sign-in, finish **onboarding** (display name, birth year) to unlock Coach Board saves, predictions persistence, and community posts.

**Community** tab: friend connections, activity, and moderation-safe posts. Fan Chat supports direct messages between connected users.

**Help** in the header menu reopens the welcome tour or lets you ask **Kickboard AI** (answers from our help knowledge) or **message an admin** for account and policy questions.

Admins can review all help conversations in **Admin → Help** when signed in with an allowlisted Google account.
