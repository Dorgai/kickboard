# Predictions

Open the **Predictions** tab on Current event. Select a match from the left list (use search on Coach Board; Predictions uses the same style picker).

For each fixture you can set:

- **Who wins?** — home, draw, or away
- **Exact score** (optional)
- **Goal scorers** (where enabled)

Save your picks before kickoff. Overview cards show your points and recent picks when signed in.

Share your pick from the prediction form when you want friends to see your call.
