# Tournament picks

On the **Predictions** tab, **Tournament picks** is separate from match-by-match picks.

You can save:

- **Champion** — team that wins the World Cup
- **Finalists** — two teams you think reach the final
- **Top goal scorer** — Golden Boot
- **Best player** — tournament MVP

Picks are stored per account for World Cup 2026 (`WC26`). Points settle when the tournament ends (settlement is applied automatically when results are available).

Fan Mode accounts cannot place predictions.
