# Predictions legal language

These clauses express the product intent for Kickboard. They must be reviewed by a lawyer admitted
in Switzerland or the EU before launch.

## Predictions and points are not gambling

Kickboard predictions are a free-to-play skill-based entertainment feature. No monetary stake is
required to participate. No cash prize is offered or implied. Points awarded are virtual, have no
monetary value, cannot be redeemed for cash, and cannot be transferred between users.

The outcome of predictions is determined partly by the user's knowledge of football, which is the
skill component. This is intended to support classification as a skill-based competition in
Switzerland, the European Union where local law permits, and applicable African and Asian
jurisdictions.

## Sponsorship prizes, future feature

When sponsor-funded prizes are activated for a tournament leaderboard, these additional conditions
apply:

1. No purchase is necessary to enter or win.
2. Entry is free for all registered users.
3. Prizes are awarded on the basis of accumulated points earned through demonstrably skill-based
   activities, such as correct score predictions, player ratings, and squad accuracy.
4. The platform operator may retain a disclosed service fee of 10-20% of the sponsor prize pool.
5. Prize fulfilment is the responsibility of the sponsoring organisation.
6. The platform operator is not a licensed gambling operator, and the promotion is not intended to
   constitute gambling under applicable law.

## Jurisdiction notes for legal review

- Switzerland: predictions without monetary stake are expected to sit outside gambling regulation
  under the Federal Act on Gambling. Confirm with ESBK before sponsor prizes.
- EU member states: definitions vary. Germany, France, and the Netherlands require specific review.
  No-purchase-necessary and skill component language are key exemptions in many markets.
- Nigeria: the National Lottery Regulatory Commission governs prize promotions. Sponsor prizes may
  require notification or registration depending on value.
- India: skill-based games are generally exempt under the Public Gambling Act 1867 and state laws,
  but state-specific review is required.
- China: do not activate predictions or prize features for mainland China without separate legal
  advice.

## Product constraints

- No prediction entry fee.
- No points purchase.
- No peer-to-peer points transfer.
- No cash-out or redemption in v1.
- No sponsor prizes until jurisdiction review is complete.
